    Hist. win % comes from scripts/backtest_moves.py: every 2024–26 NFL/FBS game whose line moved, grading the side it moved toward at the opening line (Action Network has no past Tuesday lines, so the opener stands in for the Tuesday lock). Moves of 1.5 points or less are close to a coin flip. Your contest's Tuesday number may differ from DraftKings' - check it before locking.

    Splash = your contest's line (from the Splash Lines sheet tab). When a game has one, Move, Moved to and Hist. win % are measured from it; otherwise from DraftKings' Tuesday line.

    Line is the home team's DraftKings spread (negative = home favored). Tue is Tuesday's DraftKings line - from our own Tuesday splits pull, or the Weekly Lines Tuesday pull when that's missing. Move = Line minus the lock line (Splash if present, else Tue). Bets = share of tickets, $ = share of money wagered, both Action Network's consensus. A flag locks at kickoff using the last pull before it, and is graded against the line it was flagged at. College games without splits are FCS or small-market matchups Action Network doesn't report.
