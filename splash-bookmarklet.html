Splash Lines bookmarklet

Copies every game's Splash spread from your picks page to the clipboard, ready to paste into the Splash Lines tab of the sheet. It only reads what's on your screen; it stores no password and sends nothing anywhere.

Install (once)

  1. Show Firefox's bookmarks toolbar: Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
  2. Drag this button onto the toolbar:
    Copy Splash Lines

If dragging doesn't work: right-click the toolbar → Add Bookmark…, name it "Copy Splash Lines", and paste the text from the box at the bottom of this page into the URL field.

Use (each week, after Splash posts the slate)

  1. Open your Splash picks page and scroll to the bottom once so every game has loaded.
  2. Click Copy Splash Lines. A message tells you how many games it copied.
  3. In the sheet, click cell A1 of the Splash Lines tab and paste (Ctrl+V), replacing last week's rows.

Compare the count in the message with the number of games on the page. If they differ, or it says 0, Splash has probably changed its page layout; send a new sample of one game's HTML and the bookmarklet can be updated.
